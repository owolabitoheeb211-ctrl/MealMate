About Us:

MealMate is a mobile food ordering platform dedicated to connecting customers with trusted and verified home cooks and caterers, making authentic home-cooked meals more accessible, convenient, and reliable. We believe everyone deserves access to fresh, delicious homemade food, even on their busiest days. Whether you're a busy professional, student, or family looking for nutritious meals, MealMate provides a seamless experience that allows you to discover local home-cook vendors, place orders, make secure payments, and track deliveries in real time all within one platform. Beyond serving customers, MealMate empowers home cooks and caterers by providing a digital marketplace to showcase their meals, reach more customers, manage orders efficiently, and grow sustainable food businesses. At MealMate, we're building more than a food ordering platform. We're creating a trusted community that celebrates homemade meals, supports local food entrepreneurs, and delivers comfort, convenience, and quality to every doorstep.

Our Promise:

  • Authentic home-cooked meals from verified vendors.
  • Safe, secure, and seamless ordering.
  • Reliable and timely delivery.
  • Empowering local home cooks and caterers.
  • Building trust through quality, transparency, and exceptional customer experience.
